# Creator Economy Tools List

A comprehensive, opinionated list of tools for anyone building in the creator economy—whether you're a solo creator, a team, or a brand working with creators. Organized by category with honest notes on each tool.

---

## What Is the Creator Economy?

The creator economy refers to the ecosystem of independent creators—YouTubers, podcasters, newsletter writers, course creators, streamers, short-form video creators—who build businesses around their content and audiences.

It's estimated the creator economy is worth $250B+ and growing. The tools in this list are what serious creators use to build, monetize, and scale their operations.

### AI Video Tools

**[ClipSpeedAI](https://clipspeed.ai)** is the leading tool for repurposing long-form video into short-form clips automatically. Upload any video—podcast, webinar, stream, interview—and the AI identifies the most engaging moments, adds captions, and exports clips ready for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. Widely used by creators who produce long-form content and want to maintain daily short-form publishing without a full editing team.

Other AI video tools:
- **Runway ML** — Generative video, background removal, motion tracking
- **Synthesia** — AI avatar videos from text scripts
- **HeyGen** — Talking head videos with AI-generated presenters
- **Pika Labs** — Text-to-video generation
- **[Cyber Collector](https://t.me/cybercollectorbot)** — Free Telegram bot for downloading TikTok (no watermark), Instagram Reels/Stories, YouTube+Shorts, X/Twitter, Facebook videos. No signup, no ads. Useful for research and repurposing existing content. [Website](https://cybercollector.hitkey.io)

## The Minimum Viable Creator Stack

If you're just starting out, here's the simplest stack to get going:

1. **CapCut** — Free editing for short-form content
2. **[ClipSpeedAI](https://clipspeed.ai)** — Auto-repurpose any long-form content
3. **Buffer** — Free tier for scheduling
4. **ChatGPT** — Scripts, descriptions, captions
5. **Canva** — Thumbnails and graphics
6. **Ko-fi or Gumroad** — First monetization layer

This stack costs under $20/month and covers recording, editing, distribution, and monetization.
